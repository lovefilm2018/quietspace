Property Staging

First impressions matter.

I prepare homes for sale, rental or photography by working with what you already own.

Together, we can highlight your home’s potential and create a welcoming space for prospective buyers or guests.

When can home staging help?

  • You’re preparing your property for sale.
  • You want to get your property ready for new tenants.
  • You’re preparing a holiday home for guests.
  • Your property is empty and needs a welcoming touch.
  • Your rooms feel crowded or their purpose is unclear.
  • You want to make better use of your existing furniture and accessories.
  • You’re getting ready for property photos or viewings.
  • You want help bringing the finishing touches together.

Make the most of your property.

Whether preparing a Chichester or Hampshire home for estate agent photography or styling a coastal holiday let in West Wittering or Bosham, I declutter, edit furniture flow, and add thoughtful finishing touches using what you already own (from £160).

A fresh perspective. A welcoming first impression.
