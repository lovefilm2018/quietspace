Pre-Move Decluttering & Move-In Unpacking

Settle into your new home calmly from day one.

Moving house is exciting, yet living out of cardboard boxes for months quickly drains your energy. I help you edit belongings before packing day, then unpack and organise your new kitchen, wardrobes, and living spaces into intuitive systems right away.

Whether you are relocating to Chichester, Emsworth, Arundel, Petersfield, or Haslemere, we can book around your completion date (ideally 4–6 weeks ahead where possible).

How move-in support works

  • Declutter before you pack—stop paying removals to move items you no longer use or love.
  • Label and group boxes by room and daily priority so unpacking feels calm rather than chaotic.
  • Day-one move-in unpacking: kitchens, wardrobes, and bedrooms organised so your first night feels like home.
  • Seamless coordination alongside your local removals team across Chichester, West Sussex, Hampshire, and Surrey.

Transparent day rates, zero hidden extras

Pre-move sorting and unpacking sessions are booked as Short Sessions (3–4 hours, £120–£160) or Full Days (6–10 hours, £240–£400) across Chichester, West Sussex, Hampshire, and South Surrey—with travel outside these areas considered upon request.
